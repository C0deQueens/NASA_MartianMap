How the safety score works

Every stop starts at 100. Points are taken off for the conditions below. 80 and up is Safe, 50–79 is Caution, under 50 is Danger. Slope is calculated from the elevation change and distance of the leg walked to reach the stop.

Slope
  • under 5° → 0
  • 5–10° → −10
  • 10–15° → −20
  • 15° or more → −35
Temperature
  • −40 °C or warmer → 0
  • −40 to −60 °C → −5
  • below −60 °C → −15
Visibility
  • 4 km or more → 0
  • under 4 km → −10
Terrain
  • Clay-bearing unit (sand patches) → −5
  • Greenheugh Pediment (sharp, jagged rock) → −15
  • Gediz Vallis Ridge (loose rock, boulders) → −15

Thresholds are our prototype planning values, not official NASA spacewalk limits. Coordinates, sols, elevation, temperature, UV and visibility come from the team's data sheet (Curiosity "Where is Curiosity?" map and NASA Mars Trek HiRISE DEM). Radiation uses Curiosity RAD's surface average of about 0.67 mSv/day. Walking time assumes 2 km/h in a suit. Distances are straight lines between stops, so the real walk is longer.
